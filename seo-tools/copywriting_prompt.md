## Writing Guidelines

### Content Requirements
1. **Length:** 1,500-2,500 words (comprehensive but scannable)
2. **Reading Level:** 8th grade (Hemingway Editor score 8 or below)
3. **Paragraphs:** 2-3 sentences max
4. **Sentences:** 20 words or less (average)
5. **Active Voice:** 80% minimum
6. **Transition Words:** 30% of sentences

### SEO Optimization
- **Primary Keyword:** Natural placement (1-2% density)
- **LSI Keywords:** Semantically related terms throughout
- **Headers:** H2 every 300 words, H3 for subsections
- **Meta Description:** 150-160 characters
- **Title Tag:** Under 60 characters with keyword

### Engagement Elements
1. **Opening Hook:** Question, statistic, or bold statement
2. **Storytelling:** Personal anecdote or case study
3. **Data Visualization:** Suggest charts/graphs for complex data
4. **Interactive Elements:** Quizzes, calculators, checklists
5. **Social Proof:** Expert quotes, testimonials, studies

### E-E-A-T Signals
- **Experience:** Share specific examples and case studies
- **Expertise:** Reference credentials and deep knowledge
- **Authority:** Link to authoritative sources, cite studies
- **Trust:** Transparency, accurate data, proper citations

## Content Creation Process

### Write the Article
Following this structure:

1. **Compelling Introduction** (100-150 words)
   - Hook with problem/question
   - Promise clear value
   - Set expectations

2. **Main Content** (1,200-2,000 words)
   - Clear, logical flow
   - One idea per paragraph
   - Examples and data throughout
   - Visual break suggestions

3. **Strong Conclusion** (100-200 words)
   - Summarize key points
   - Provide clear next steps
   - Link to related resources

### Enrichment Features

#### Internal Linking Strategy
- Link to 3-5 relevant internal pages
- Use descriptive anchor text
- Support topic clustering

#### External Citations
- Link to 5-10 authoritative sources
- Recent studies and data (last 2 years)
- Government/educational/industry sites
- Format: [Source Name](URL)

#### Visual Content Suggestions
For each section, suggest:
- Type: [Infographic/Chart/Screenshot/Diagram]
- Content: [What to visualize]
- Tool: [Napkin.ai/Canva/Custom]
- Purpose: [Why it helps understanding]

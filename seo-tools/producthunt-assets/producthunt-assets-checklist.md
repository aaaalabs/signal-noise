## 🎨 **ASSET CREATION STRATEGY**

### **Design Consistency**
- **Color Palette**: Black (#000000), Signal green (#00ff88), white text
- **Typography**: San Francisco font family, weights 100/300/500
- **Styling**: Consistent with blog and app aesthetic
- **Branding**: Match Jony Ive design principles throughout

### **Content Hierarchy**
1. **Logo**: Immediate brand recognition
2. **Hero Screenshot**: Show main value (signal ratio tracking)
3. **Method Screenshot**: Show unique approach (binary classification)
4. **Results Screenshot**: Show transformation (before/after data)
5. **Features Screenshots**: Supporting functionality
6. **Demo Video**: Bring everything together in motion

### **Mobile-First Approach**
- **All screenshots**: iPhone/mobile device frames
- **Portrait orientation**: ProductHunt community mostly mobile
- **Touch interactions**: Show finger taps and gestures
- **Readable text**: Large enough for mobile viewing

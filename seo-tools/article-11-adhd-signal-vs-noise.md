## The ADHD Signal/Noise Implementation Guide

### Morning Signal Identification (The Dopamine Prime)

**Step 1: The 3-Signal Rule**
- Identify exactly 3 signal tasks for today
- Write them on a physical card (not digital)
- Place card in direct line of sight

**Step 2: Everything Else Is Noise**
- Don't prioritize noise tasks
- Don't organize noise tasks
- Simply acknowledge: "That's noise"

**Step 3: Signal Sprint Protocol**
- Work on signal until hyperfocus breaks
- When it breaks, take a real break
- Return to signal, not noise

### The ADHD-Optimized Daily Schedule

```
6 AM - 10 AM: SIGNAL BLOCK (peak dopamine)
├─ No email
├─ No meetings
├─ No "quick questions"
└─ Pure signal execution

10 AM - 12 PM: CONTROLLED NOISE
├─ Batch all noise tasks
├─ Set hard stop at noon
└─ No signal work allowed

12 PM - 1 PM: MOVEMENT BREAK
└─ Physical activity to reset dopamine

1 PM - 4 PM: SIGNAL BLOCK II
└─ Second highest-value signal

4 PM - 5 PM: NOISE CLEANUP
└─ Tomorrow's signal identification
```

### Tools and Environmental Design

**Digital Environment**:

## Common ADHD Pitfalls and Solutions

### Pitfall 1: "Everything Feels Like Signal"

**Solution**: The 10-10-10 Rule
- Will this matter in 10 minutes? (Probably noise)
- Will this matter in 10 months? (Maybe signal)
- Will this matter in 10 years? (Definitely signal)

### Pitfall 2: Hyperfocus on Wrong Target

**Solution**: Signal Validation Questions
1. Does this directly advance my primary goal?
2. Am I doing this because it's interesting or important?
3. Would Steve Jobs spend time on this?

### Pitfall 3: Noise Guilt

**Solution**: Noise is necessary, just contained
- 20% noise is optimal, not a failure
- Schedule noise time to reduce guilt
- Batch noise to preserve signal blocks

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is signal vs noise better than medication for ADHD?
Signal vs noise is a productivity methodology, not a medical treatment. It works alongside whatever ADHD management strategy you use, whether that includes medication, therapy, or lifestyle changes. Many find it reduces the need for complex organizational systems that often fail with ADHD.

### How do I identify signal when everything seems important?
Use the "Steve Jobs Test": If you could only complete 3 tasks before your company shuts down, which would you choose? Those are signal. Everything else, no matter how it feels, is noise. ADHD brains often struggle with importance inflation—the binary framework cuts through this.

### Can signal vs noise work with ADHD time blindness?
Yes. Instead of time-based planning, use energy-based signal blocks. Work on signal during high-dopamine periods (usually morning), regardless of time. The framework adapts to your natural rhythms rather than forcing arbitrary schedules.

### What if my hyperfocus targets the wrong things?
Pre-commit to signal tasks in writing before starting work. Physical cards work better than digital for ADHD brains. Once hyperfocus engages, you're already locked onto signal. It's about aiming the weapon, not controlling the trigger.

### How does this differ from other ADHD productivity methods?
Unlike complex ADHD systems (bullet journaling, elaborate color coding), signal vs noise requires just one decision per task. No maintenance, no reviews, no elaborate setups. It's designed for ADHD reality: inconsistent execution, variable energy, and decision fatigue.

### Should I tell my employer about using this method?
Focus on results, not methods. As your signal ratio improves, your output will speak for itself. Many ADHD professionals find that succeeding with this method reduces the need for workplace accommodations.

### Can this help with ADHD paralysis?
Yes. Analysis paralysis occurs when there are too many options. Binary choice (signal or noise) bypasses the paralysis trigger. When stuck, ask: "Is this signal?" If yes, start. If no, ignore. No third option.

### What about ADHD rejection sensitive dysphoria (RSD)?
Saying no to noise tasks might trigger RSD initially. Remember: Jobs said no to good ideas to say yes to great ones. Frame it as "protecting your signal" rather than "rejecting requests."

### How long before I see results?
Most ADHD professionals report noticeable improvements within 3-5 days. The immediate reduction in decision fatigue provides quick wins. Full habit formation takes 20-30 days, but the binary nature makes it easier to maintain than complex systems.

### Is this just another productivity fad for ADHD?
No. This is based on information theory (Claude Shannon), proven by Steve Jobs, and validated by neuroscience research on ADHD decision-making. It's not about working harder—it's about working with your brain's architecture, not against it.

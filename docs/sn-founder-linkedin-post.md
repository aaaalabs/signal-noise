# Signal/Noise LinkedIn Post - Founder Story

As a startup founder, I was drowning in my own to-do list.

Every morning: Journal. Plan. Prioritize.
Every evening: "What did I actually accomplish?"

The brutal truth? Almost nothing that moved my business forward.

I had 40+ "important" tasks.
But only 3 actually generated revenue or growth.

The rest? Pure noise.

**The problem with journaling**: It helps you reflect, but doesn't force you to choose.

**The problem with most productivity systems**: They organize noise better, but don't eliminate it.

So I built something different.

An app that forces one simple question:
→ **SIGNAL** (moves business forward) or **NOISE** (everything else)?

No categories. No priorities. No complexity.
Just: Does this matter for my startup's survival?

Then I added an AI that holds me accountable.
→ "You said this was signal yesterday. Did you ship it?"
→ "You've marked 5 things as signal today. Pick 2."
→ "This looks like noise disguised as work."

**Results after 3 months:**
→ My "important" list went from 40 to 8 items
→ I complete 3x more business-critical tasks
→ My AI calls out my excuses (like a good co-founder would)

**For startup founders:**
We don't need better organization.
We need better decision-making.
We need accountability.
We need someone to call BS on our "busy work."

Most founders spend 80% of their time on tasks that contribute 0% to business success.

Signal/Noise fixes that.

**It's free. Your data stays private. Works in German & English.**

Because every founder deserves a co-founder that calls out their noise.

Try it: signal-noise.app

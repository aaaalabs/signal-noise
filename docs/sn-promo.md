## Technical Specs

### Duration
- **Optimal**: 25 seconds
- **Maximum**: 30 seconds
- **Hook minimum**: 3 seconds to grab attention

### Visual Style
- **Dark theme** throughout (matches app)
- **Minimal animations** (Jony Ive style)
- **Green accent** for signals (brand color)
- **Clean typography** (system fonts, weight hierarchy)

### Audio
- **No background music** (distracting)
- **Clean voiceover** with confident delivery
- **Sound effects**: Subtle click/completion sounds
- **Silence moments**: Let the Jobs quote breathe

### Platform Optimizations
- **9:16 ratio** for TikTok/Instagram Stories
- **16:9 version** for YouTube/LinkedIn
- **Square version** for Instagram Feed
- **Captions always on** (accessibility + mobile)

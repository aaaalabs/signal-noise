💡 How to Use This Matrix

Step 1: List all tasks competing for your attention

Step 2: Run each task through the 4 questions in order

Step 3: Take action based on first "Yes" answer:
• Q1=Yes → Eliminate (stop doing it)
• Q2=Yes → Automate (set up system/tool)
• Q3=Yes → Delegate (assign to team member)
• Q4=Yes → Batch (schedule specific time block)
• All No → Transformational work = Your Three Things

Step 4: Protect your Three Things ruthlessly - everything else has been handled

📝 Examples by Category
ELIMINATE:
• Meetings without clear purpose
• Reports nobody reads
• "FYI" email chains
• Redundant approval steps

• Social media posting

DELEGATE:

BATCH:
• Email responses (2× daily)

- ✅ Under 125 characters for most (shorter alternatives provided)
- ✅ Contextually relevant to surrounding content
- ✅ Accessible for screen readers

### Keyword Density per Image:
- Primary keyword ("three things") appears in 6/11 images
- Secondary keywords distributed across all images
- Long-tail keywords in infographic alt text

- All alt text describes visual content accurately

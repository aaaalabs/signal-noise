Steve Jobs Stanford Commencement Speech 2005 - Productivity & Focus Excerpts

TIME MANAGEMENT QUOTES:
"If today were the last day of my life, would I want to do what I am about to do today?"

"Your time is limited, so don't waste it living someone else's life. Don't be trapped by dogma — which is living with the results of other people's thinking. Don't let the noise of others' opinions drown out your own inner voice."

FOCUS & PASSION:
"The only way to do great work is to love what you do. If you haven't found it yet, keep looking. Don't settle."

"Keep looking. Don't settle."

LIFE PHILOSOPHY:
"Stay hungry, stay foolish."

"Remembering that you are going to die is the best way I know to avoid the trap of thinking you have something to lose. You are already naked. There is no reason not to follow your heart."

PRODUCTIVITY INSIGHTS:
- Emphasized following curiosity and intuition over conventional wisdom
- Viewed setbacks (being fired from Apple) as opportunities for growth and learning
- Recommended trusting inner voice over external expectations and noise
- Connected passion with productivity: great work only comes from loving what you do

SIGNAL VS NOISE ELEMENTS:
- "Don't let the noise of others' opinions drown out your own inner voice"
- Focus on what truly matters (your passion and purpose)
- Eliminate external distractions and dogma
- Time is finite - use it on what matters most

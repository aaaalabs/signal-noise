## ⚖️ **Legal Framework for SEO Content**

### **Fair Use Guidelines for Blog Content**

#### **The Four Factor Test**
1. **Purpose**: Educational/commentary use favored over commercial
2. **Nature**: Factual content better than creative works
3. **Amount**: Small portions preferred
4. **Market Effect**: Must not substitute for original

#### **2024 Legal Updates**
- **AI and Copyright**: New precedents from NYT v. OpenAI case
- **Transformative Use**: Higher standards after Warhol v. Goldsmith ruling
- **Educational Use**: Still requires careful case-by-case analysis

### **Best Practices for SEO Agents**
1. **Document Analysis**: Record fair use decision rationale
2. **Seek Permission**: Direct contact when possible
3. **Attribution**: Proper credit even when not legally required
4. **Alternative Sources**: Prioritize Creative Commons and public domain

### **Safe Harbor Strategies**
- **Original Creation**: AI generation eliminates copyright concerns
- **Licensed Content**: Purchase appropriate licenses for commercial use
- **Public Domain**: Historical content with expired copyrights
- **Creative Commons**: Proper attribution following license terms

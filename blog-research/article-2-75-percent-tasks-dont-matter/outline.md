- **Origins**: Vilfredo Pareto's wealth distribution discovery

- **Real Examples**:

### IV. Busy vs. Productive: The Critical Distinction (350 words)
- **Busy Work Characteristics**:
  - Reactive instead of proactive
  - High visibility, low impact
  - Easily measurable but ultimately meaningless
- **Productive Work Traits**:
  - Aligns with core objectives
  - Creates lasting value
  - Often invisible to others
- **The Interruption Epidemic**: 23 minutes 15 seconds to refocus
- Context-switching costs: 4 hours weekly just refocusing

### V. How to Identify Your 25% (400 words)
- **The Impact Audit**:
  - Track tasks for one week
  - Rate each on 1-10 impact scale
  - Identify patterns in high-impact activities
- **Three Questions Framework**:
  1. Does this move me toward my core goals?
  2. Would anything break if I didn't do this?
  3. Am I the only one who can do this?
- **The Eisenhower Matrix**: Urgent vs. Important distinction
- **Energy Mapping**: Align high-energy times with high-impact tasks

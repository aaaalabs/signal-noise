Key Insights from Daniel Pink's "When" Research
Peak Energy (8-11am): Best for analytical, strategic thinking. Allocate most demanding transformational task here.
Post-Lunch Trough (12-2pm): Energy dips after lunch. Use 1-2pm for execution-focused (not creative) work.
Recovery Period (2-5pm): Declining cognitive capacity. Perfect for batched maintenance and planning.
Breaks are Strategic: 15-minute breaks between blocks prevent attention residue and maintain focus quality.
Total Deep Work: 6 hours matches Dr. David Rock's finding that we're truly focused only ~6 hours/week. This system maximizes those precious hours.

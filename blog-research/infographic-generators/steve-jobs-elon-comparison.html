Jobs Methodology: Sustainable Excellence
Steve Jobs achieved 80% signal through systematic noise elimination. His approach emphasized quality over quantity, deep focus over busy work. The 18-hour window allowed for both signal execution and essential recovery.
"Jobs' vision of Signal was the top 3 to 5 things you have to get done in the next 18 hours"
— Kevin O'Leary, The Diary of a CEO
Musk Methodology: Maximum Intensity
Elon Musk operates at 100% signal through extreme time optimization. 5-minute time boxing eliminates all noise, but requires exceptional discipline. This approach maximizes output but demands sustainable personal systems.
"The geniuses of their time were close to 100% signal"
— Kevin O'Leary, The Diary of a CEO
